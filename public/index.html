002
StackPath 2021–2023 Cloud Platform Component System

Design System at Scale

At StackPath (a cloud computing platform) I designed and built a reusable component library consumed by engineers across customer-facing and internal product surfaces. Documented in Storybook, implemented in code, aligned across teams through shared tokens and standards.

The Problem

A fast-growing engineering team with no unified component language. Inconsistency compounding with every release across multiple surfaces.

The Work

Component library built from the ground up in Figma and code. Storybook documentation for engineering adoption. Shared tokens, patterns, and standards that scaled.

The Impact

A single source of truth for engineers. Consistent UI across products. Design and development aligned through standards, not repeated conversations.

I'm a product designer with a frontend development background, currently working at United Airlines as the senior designer on a hybrid 6-person UX and engineering team. I design operational tools for pilots and crew at scale and I build what I design.

My work lives in dense, complex environments where information architecture is mission critical. I implement in Angular, React, and SCSS, collaborate inside the codebase with engineers, and use GitHub Copilot to move faster without losing quality.

My eye for visual craft comes from a background in photography, studied at The Art Institute, and it's shaped how I think about composition, hierarchy, and the weight of every element on screen. I'm currently pursuing a Multimedia Programming & Design degree at BMCC with the goal of transferring to NYU Tisch's IMA program, where interaction, media, and technology intersect. Long term I'm building toward a head of product design role.
